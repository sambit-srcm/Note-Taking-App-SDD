## Change

A history panel that lists versions and restores one.

## Why

FRS-VERSION-001 and FRS-VERSION-002 need the owner to inspect a snapshot and put it back.

## Scope

The panel on an existing note lists versions and posts a restore. It is not on the public share page.

## Out of scope

Version API changes.

## FRS ids

FRS-VERSION-001, FRS-VERSION-002.

## Open decisions

None.
