## Change

A share panel on the editor and a public read-only page.

## Why

FRS-SHARE-001 needs the owner to create and revoke a link, and a visitor to read the note without edit or history.

## Scope

The panel creates a 7-day link, shows the token once, and revokes it. `/shared/:token` shows the title and the words "Read only".

## Out of scope

Share API changes and view-count cookies, which the API already sets.

## FRS ids

FRS-SHARE-001, FRS-SHARE-002.

## Open decisions

None.
